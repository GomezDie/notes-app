# 001 Nobody shot me

**Hook (on screen):** SHOT 14 TIMES. HIS LAST WORDS:
**Length:** 66 s (narration 64 s)

## Narration
February fourteenth, nineteen twenty-nine. Chicago. Seven men are waiting inside a brick garage on North Clark Street. A black Cadillac pulls up outside. It looks exactly like a police car. And two of the four men who step out are wearing police uniforms. Inside, nobody panics. In Prohibition Chicago, raids happen all the time. So the seven men do what they're told. They line up facing the back wall. Then the officers open fire. Two Thompson submachine guns. Two shotguns. Around seventy rounds in a matter of seconds. When the real police arrive, one man is still alive. Frank Gusenberg. He has fourteen bullets in him. A detective asks him who shot him. "Nobody shot me," he says. Three hours later, he's dead. He never names a single killer. To this day, no one has ever been convicted for the Saint Valentine's Day Massacre. But the man everyone blamed would one day fall... thanks to a nervous bookkeeper.
